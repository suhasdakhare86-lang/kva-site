Our Story

A working farm, opened up for you to slow down in.

Kshanbhar Vishranti — "a moment of rest" — is a family-run agrotourism farm at Village Dhanora, about 25 km from Rajura via the Rajura–Sindhi Road (nearest town, Virur, is just 5 km away). Started in 2019, the farm grows pomegranate, custard apple, seasonal vegetables and fodder, and keeps indigenous cattle, goats and country poultry.

Our aim is simple: give city families and school groups an honest, hands-on taste of farm life, run on organic principles — while creating steady, year-round work for people from Dhanora village. We're registered under the Maharashtra Agri-Tourism Policy.
