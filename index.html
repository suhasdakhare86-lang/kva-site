Our Story

A working farm, opened up for your family for the day.

Kshanbhar Vishranti — "a moment of rest" — is a family-run farm in Dhanora village, near Virur Station in Rajura taluka, Chandrapur district, sitting between the Tadoba and Jogapur forest areas. The farm grows jowar (jwari), tur and sunflower, keeps orchards and pet animals, and the sunflower field is our best-loved spot for photos.

We host one kind of visit — a full day on the farm, 10:30 AM to 5 PM, with a farm tour, activities and vegetarian meals included. Our staff speak Marathi, Hindi, English and Telugu.
