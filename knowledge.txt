KNOWLEDGE BASE

Overview
Kshanbhar Vishranti Agro Tourism is a family-run agro tourism and water park destination in Dhanora, near Virur Station, Rajura taluka, Chandrapur district, Maharashtra 442905, run by Sharda Eknath Dakhare. Our three main attractions are the sunflower farm, agro tourism experiences, and a large water park. The farm grows jowar (jwari), tur and sunflower, has an orchard of about 100 fruit plants, a unique plant collection, pet animals and a bird house. Staff speak Marathi, Hindi, English and Telugu. Instagram: @kshanbhar_vishranti_at.

Location and how to reach
The farm is in Dhanora village near Virur Station, Rajura, Chandrapur 442905, between the Tadoba and Jogapur forest areas. Rajura town is 20 km (30 min) via Sindhi Road or Virur Station Road. Ballarpur, the nearest railway station, is 28 km (40 min). Chandrapur is 45 km (1 hr 30 min) via the Chandrapur-Rajura highway. Nagpur airport is 150 km (4 hr) via the Nagpur-Chandrapur highway. Parking is available for cars, buses and two-wheelers. The farm does not provide pickup or drop. The approach road stays in good condition during monsoon.

Timings
The farm is open for day visits from 10:30 AM to 5 PM. School trips run 11 AM to 5 PM on weekdays only. There is no overnight stay.

Day picnic package
The full-day picnic runs 10:30 AM to 5 PM and costs Rs 700 per adult and Rs 400 per child below 10 years. The price includes meals, the water park, and all other activities (farming, sports, kids/adult play areas, magic show, selfie points). Food is pure vegetarian. There is no weekend or festival surcharge. Half-day and overnight packages are not offered.

Group and corporate package
Groups of 20 or more people get 10% off the adult price: Rs 630 per adult instead of Rs 700. Timing is 10:30 AM to 5 PM with meals, the water park and activities included. Corporate groups can book the hall and projector at extra cost; games are free.

School and college trips
School and college trips cost Rs 500 per student, run 11 AM to 5 PM, and are available on weekdays only, not weekends. The price includes meals, an educational farm tour and activities, with safety staff present. Up to 500 students can be hosted.

Birthdays and family functions
The farm hosts birthdays and family functions for up to 500 guests, starting at Rs 700 per person. Decoration, cake and sound system are arranged at extra cost.

Food
Food is pure vegetarian and included in the package: a welcome drink, breakfast with tea, an afternoon refreshment, seasonal hurda served with bharit and thecha, lunch of chapati/bhakri, zunka, aloo/vangi bhaji, kavtachi chatni, a sweet, chinch-chi kadhi and chatni, and evening tea. Non-veg is not served and outside food is not allowed.

Safety and facilities
RO drinking water and first aid are available on the farm. The nearest hospital is Virur Government Hospital. The farm is safe for small children and elderly guests but is not wheelchair accessible. Mobile network is available.

Booking
Book by WhatsApp message and pay a 10% advance by UPI or bank transfer. Steps: guest shares date, package and number of adults and children; farm confirms availability and total; guest pays 10% advance; farm sends confirmation on WhatsApp.

Policies
No refund on cancellation. Date change is not allowed. Alcohol, smoking, pets, loud music / DJ and outside food are not allowed. For damage to property the guest pays 100% of the repair or replacement cost.

Nature and nearby places
The farm sits between the Tadoba and Jogapur forest areas. Jogapur forest, the Wardha river and Dongargaon dam are nearby, and guests can see wildlife and greenery on the road to the farm.

Water Park
Our water park spans 25,000 square feet with 18 separate water activities for kids and adults. It has a multi-play station with open, closed, curved, family and children's slides, plus the Water Daku slide, separate swimming pools for adults and children, water boating, and separate rain-dance areas for adults and children. Clean washrooms and changing rooms are provided.

Boating
A separate boating pool is available for both adults and children to enjoy a boat ride.

Farm & sunflower experience
Guests can visit our seasonal sunflower field for photos, and enjoy photo sessions across the farm. We have around 100 fruit plants on the property and serve seasonal fruit to guests based on availability.

Sports & adventure activities
We offer a variety of rural and advanced sports, plus an underground play area where kids and adults can play undisturbed. Adventure activities include a Burma Bridge walk, a zigzag bridge walk, a tyre bridge, ghasarpatti (a traditional slide), a zulla (swing), a commando net, a cycling track, and a walking track along a palm-tree-lined road.

Kids activities
Kids activities include: a swimming pool with swimming tubes, rain dance, archery (dhanushban), children's golf, carrom board, dart board, kids basketball, children's group cricket, lagori, and hopper ball (bounce rubber ball games).

Adult activities
Adult activities include: a swimming pool, rain dance, family group cricket, badminton, volleyball, kabaddi, kho-kho, rassi khech (tug of war), indoor games, many selfie points, old traditional games, bullock cart riding, and carrom board.

Magic show & selfie points
We host a magic show with a variety of magic tricks, and have a variety of selfie points across the property for photos.

Our speciality
Our specialities include the sunflower farm in bloom, a unique plant collection, organic fruit from the farm, a natural experience with farm animals, traditional food, special evening snacks with a sunset view, a mud/soil dance experience, the natural sound of birds, and a bird house with a variety of birds.

Bullock cart riding policy
Bullock cart riding is included for guests and is free for Indian Army families (a valid ID card is required). International tourists must show a valid visa and passport on arrival. All guests must accept the consent form at the reception desk.

PRECAUTIONS

General farm safety
 precautions: wear closed footwear, stay on marked paths, watch children near water and animals, do not enter animal enclosures. Emergency: call coordinator, ambulance 108.

Around farm animals
Animal safety: approach farm animals only with a guide, move slowly, do not stand behind bullocks, do not run or shout, wash hands after touching animals, supervise children.

Near the farm pond and canal
Water safety: farm pond up to 8 feet deep, no swimming or wading, stay behind fencing, hold children near water, ring buoy on viewing deck, emergency 108.
