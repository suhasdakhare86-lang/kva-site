=== KNOWLEDGE BASE ===

Overview
Overview
Kshanbhar Vishranti Agro Tourism is a 15-acre family farm in Dhanora, near Virur Station, Rajura taluka, Chandrapur district, Maharashtra 442905, run by Sharda Eknath Dakhare. The farm grows jowar (jwari), tur and sunflower, has orchards and pet animals, and its sunflower field is the main attraction. Staff speak Marathi, Hindi, English and Telugu. Instagram: @kshanbhar_vishranti_at.

Location and how to reach
Location and how to reach
The farm is in Dhanora village near Virur Station, Rajura, Chandrapur 442905, between the Tadoba and Jogapur forest areas. Rajura town is 20 km (30 min) via Sindhi Road or Virur Station Road. Ballarpur, the nearest railway station, is 28 km (40 min). Chandrapur is 45 km (1 hr 30 min) via the Chandrapur-Rajura highway. Nagpur airport is 150 km (4 hr) via the Nagpur-Chandrapur highway. Parking is available for cars, buses and two-wheelers. The farm does not provide pickup or drop. The approach road stays in good condition during monsoon.

Timings
Timings
The farm is open for day visits from 10:30 AM to 5 PM. School trips run 11 AM to 5 PM on weekdays only. There is no overnight stay.

Day picnic package
Day picnic package
The full-day picnic runs 10:30 AM to 5 PM and costs Rs 700 per adult and Rs 400 per child below 10 years. The price includes meals and all included activities. Food is pure vegetarian. There is no weekend or festival surcharge. Half-day and overnight packages are not offered.

Group and corporate package
Group and corporate package
Groups of 20 or more people get 10% off the adult price: Rs 630 per adult instead of Rs 700. Timing is 10:30 AM to 5 PM with meals and activities included. Corporate groups can book the hall and projector at extra cost; games are free.

School and college trips
School and college trips
School and college trips cost Rs 500 per student, run 11 AM to 5 PM, and are available on weekdays only, not weekends. The price includes meals, an educational farm tour and activities, with safety staff present. Up to 500 students can be hosted.

Birthdays and family functions
Birthdays and family functions
The farm hosts birthdays and family functions for up to 500 guests, starting at Rs 700 per person. Decoration, cake and sound system are arranged at extra cost.

Activities
Activities
Activities included in the package price: farm tour and crop picking, bullock cart ride, swimming / rain dance, campfire, indoor and village games, cow milking and animal feeding, and sunflower field visit and photos. Tractor ride is not available.

Food
Food
All food is pure vegetarian and included in the package. Non-veg is not served. Outside food is not allowed. Jain or no-onion-garlic meals are not available. Welcome drink / breakfast 10 AM to 12 noon, lunch 2 PM to 5 PM, evening tea and snacks at 5 PM.

Safety and facilities
Safety and facilities
RO drinking water and first aid are available on the farm. The nearest hospital is Virur Government Hospital. The farm is safe for small children and elderly guests but is not wheelchair accessible. Mobile network is available.

Booking
Booking
Book by WhatsApp message and pay a 10% advance by UPI or bank transfer. Steps: guest shares date, package and number of adults and children; farm confirms availability and total; guest pays 10% advance; farm sends confirmation on WhatsApp.

Policies
Policies
No refund on cancellation. Date change is not allowed. Alcohol, smoking, pets, loud music / DJ and outside food are not allowed. For damage to property the guest pays 100% of the repair or replacement cost.

Nature and nearby places
Nature and nearby places
The farm sits between the Tadoba and Jogapur forest areas. Jogapur forest, the Wardha river and Dongargaon dam are nearby, and guests can see wildlife and greenery on the road to the farm.

=== PRECAUTIONS ===

General farm safety
General farm safety precautions: wear closed footwear, stay on marked paths, watch children near water and animals, do not enter animal enclosures. Emergency: call coordinator, ambulance 108.

Around farm animals
Animal safety: approach farm animals only with a guide, move slowly, do not stand behind bullocks, do not run or shout, wash hands after touching animals, supervise children.

Near the farm pond and canal
Water safety: farm pond up to 8 feet deep, no swimming or wading, stay behind fencing, hold children near water, ring buoy on viewing deck, emergency 108.
