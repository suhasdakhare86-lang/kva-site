=== PRECAUTIONS ===

General farm safety
General farm safety precautions: wear closed footwear, stay on marked paths, watch children near water and animals, do not enter animal enclosures. Emergency: call coordinator, ambulance 108.

Around farm animals
Animal safety: approach farm animals only with a guide, move slowly, do not stand behind bullocks, do not run or shout, wash hands after touching animals, supervise children.

Near the farm pond and canal
Water safety: farm pond up to 8 feet deep, no swimming or wading, stay behind fencing, hold children near water, ring buoy on viewing deck, emergency 108.
